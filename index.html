For each river and year, up to 15 fish are randomly selected as candidates, with a target of 10 imaged fish per age class. The remaining candidates are kept as backups in case some scales are unsuitable for imaging.

Required = the fixed target of 10 fish per river, year, and age class.

Imaged = selected fish that have a scale image on file

Excess = imaged fish beyond the target of 10. These are not needed to reach 100% coverage but provide additional data.

Data are refreshed daily from the project source spreadsheet.
