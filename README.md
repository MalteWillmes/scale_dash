# NASCO Salmon Growth — Sample Progress

**https://maltewillmes.github.io/scale_dash/** · NINA project 132668

Shows how far the project has come in imaging salmon scales: for each river and year, how many samples are needed, how many have been imaged, and how many extra. The page is updated automatically during the working day; "Data as of" in the top right says when.

## What you see

- **Tiles:** Required, Imaged, % imaged, Excess and number of rivers for the current selection.
- **Filters:** Age class (1SW / 2SW / combined) · Institution (NINA / RB / all) · Years (last 10 / last 5 / all) · Region (Midt / Nord / Sør / Vest). Most of the page follows them.
- **Samples by year:** required, imaged and excess per sampling year.
- **Images added per day:** new images each day since tracking began. The number already imaged at the start is stated above the chart rather than drawn.
- **Map:** one circle per river. Colour is the region, size is the share imaged. Click a circle for its numbers. Positions are approximate, not sampling sites.
- **Progress by region** and **Progress by river:** the same numbers in tables. Click a river to see it year by year.

## What the numbers mean

- **Required:** the fish to image for a river, year and age class — all the fish selected, up to 10.
- **Imaged:** fish with a scale image, counted toward Required.
- **Excess:** imaged fish beyond Required. Never part of the percentage.
- **Institution:** NINA's own imaging, or Rådgivende Biologer (RB).
